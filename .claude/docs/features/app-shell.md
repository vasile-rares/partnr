# The app shell (sidebar, menus, theme)

Plan of the redesign: [ui-redesign.md](../ui-redesign.md). Where `/` sends someone and the workspace pages: [workspaces.md](workspaces.md).

## Web app

- **App shell** (`src/components/shell/`): every signed-in page sits in a sidebar. `AppShell` (server) loads the user, the workspaces they work in and the ones they are a client of, and renders `AppSidebar` (client: it needs `usePathname`, so it only receives serializable data, `ShellContext` in `types.ts`). Two layouts feed it: `app/w/[workspaceId]/layout.tsx` (`kind: "workspace"`, with up to 5 Active projects; for a client, who opens the same address, the client's menu instead, `kind: "client"`, with the projects shared with them) and `app/(shell)/layout.tsx` (`kind: "none"`: `/account`, `/workspaces`, `/workspaces/new`).
- The menus are in `nav.ts` (the firm's has Home, Projects, Clients and Tasks under "Platform", Messages and Calendar under "Collaborate" (meetings live on the Calendar), and Team under "Manage"; the client's is its own, without titles: Home, Projects, Messages, Calendar): an item without `href` is a future feature and shows disabled with a "Soon" badge, so turning one on means giving it an `href`. `ContextSwitcher` (workspaces / "Client of") is the sidebar header, `NavUser` the footer: its menu has a header (avatar, name, email), Account, a Theme row (`ThemeMenuItem` in `theme-toggle.tsx`: Light / Dark / System as icon tabs, the menu stays open, ←/→ change it from the keyboard) and Sign out. The sign-in pages keep `ThemeToggle`, a button with the radio list.
- **The sidebar is the `inset` variant** (`<Sidebar variant="inset">`): the page is a rounded card (`rounded-2xl`, 12 px margin) inside the sidebar's frame, as a continuation of it. From `md` up the card has the height of the window minus its margins and **scrolls by itself** (`AppShell` passes the classes to `SidebarInset`), so the window never scrolls and the sticky top bar stays under the card's rounded corners; on a phone the sidebar is a sheet and the page is full width and scrolls as usual. Code that has to scroll the page scrolls `<main id="main-content">`, not the window.
- **The sidebar is a little bigger than shadcn's default**, edited on purpose in `ui/sidebar.tsx` (a later `shadcn add sidebar --overwrite` would undo it): 18 rem wide (`SIDEBAR_WIDTH`, the same as the phone's sheet), menu rows 36 px high (the workspace and user buttons 52 px), 15 px text, 18 px icons and 13 px group labels, and a 200 ms colour fade on a menu row's hover and active state; a 4 px gap between menu rows (the collapsed rail keeps its 32 px buttons) so a hovered row does not touch the selected one.
- **The sidebar collapses to a rail of icons** (`collapsible="icon"`, Ctrl+B or the edge toggle; the choice is kept in a cookie), it never disappears on a wide screen. The toggle is `SidebarEdgeToggle`: a short vertical line just inside the page card, next to the sidebar, vertically centred, that bends into a `<` (or `>` when collapsed) on hover and focus; it is hidden on a phone. The header is a row: the workspace switcher and, on its right, the `NotificationsBell`; collapsed, the row is a column (switcher avatar, bell). Every menu button has a `tooltip`, so the names show on hover while only the icons are there. On a phone the sidebar is a sheet, so there (`WhenSidebarHidden`, `SidebarVisibility.tsx`) a `SidebarTrigger` button and the bell are in the top bar of the page instead, and the sheet's header is just the switcher.
- `Page.tsx`: top bar with the breadcrumb (and, on a phone only, the toggle and the bell, see above; [notifications.md](notifications.md)), then the content column; `narrow` for forms.
- Dark is the default theme (`theme-provider.tsx`).
- **Skip link and bar colour**: `AppShell` starts with a "Skip to content" link (visible only on keyboard focus) that moves focus to the `<main>` (`id="main-content"`). Each page's `<h1>` lives in its view component, not in `Page`. `ThemeColor` (root layout) keeps `<meta name="theme-color">` equal to the `--background` of the theme in use, for the browser bar on phones; the hex values are in `theme-color.tsx` and must follow `globals.css`.
- **Loading**: `loading.tsx` in `app/w/[workspaceId]/`, its `settings/` and `app/(shell)/` show `PageSkeleton` (`components/layout/`: the same top bar and column as `Page`, grey blocks inside) while a page's data loads; `narrow` matches `Page`'s. A new route that looks very different from a list can get its own `loading.tsx`.
- **Errors**: `error.tsx` in `app/`, `app/w/[workspaceId]/` and `app/(shell)/` render `ErrorState` ("Try again" calls Next's `retry`); the last two keep the sidebar (they wrap it in `Page`). `app/global-error.tsx` is the last resort, with its own `<html>`. Expected failures (a form, a row action) stay inline next to what failed, through `FormError` / the list's `message`.
- **Toasts** (`sonner`, `<Toaster />` in the root layout, bottom right): a short confirmation after a mutation whose result is not otherwise visible (archive, delete, upload, share). The row actions of the project lists get theirs from `toastDone(noun, method, path)` (`lib/toast.ts`), which reads the verb from the last segment of the path sent to `run`; other places call `toast.success(...)` themselves. Errors are not toasts.
- In e2e tests the sidebar's links are under `mainNav(page)` and the breadcrumb under `breadcrumb(page)` (`e2e/helpers.ts`); project names also appear in the sidebar, so look for links inside `getByRole("main")`.
