# Team (members and their invitations)

Who may be in a workspace and how they get there. How a client gets in is [invitations.md](invitations.md); the workspace itself is [workspaces.md](workspaces.md). Decisions: `decisions.md`, "Echipa".

## API

- **Members** (`/api/workspaces/{id}/members`, `MemberService`): `GET` is open to any member (the owner first, then by the day they joined; each with the account's name, or its email when it has none, the email, the role and `joinedAtUtc`), `DELETE {userId}` is **owner-only** and answers 204.
- **Removing** answers 409 for the owner (a workspace keeps its single owner) and for a member who still organises an upcoming, not cancelled meeting of the workspace ("X still organises 2 upcoming meetings. Cancel them before removing this person."): their Google events live in their own calendar and follow every change, with the client as a guest. Nothing is cancelled automatically, since Google would email the client. The removed account loses the workspace at once (404 on the next request); what they wrote stays under their name. They can be invited again.
- **Invitations** (`/api/workspaces/{id}/member-invitations`, `MemberInvitationService`, **all owner-only**: 403 for a member, 404 for a stranger): `POST {email}` sends one (201), `GET` lists the open ones (`Pending` or `Expired`, newest first), `DELETE {invitationId}` cancels (204; cancelling twice is fine, an accepted one answers 409). Sending again to an address with an open invitation cancels it first (a unique index allows one open invitation per address and workspace), so only the newest link works; that is also how it is resent.
- At sending, the address is refused with 409 when it already belongs to a member, or to an account that is a client of the workspace (a client sees only their own projects and a member everything, so they cannot be both); a client card without an account is fine. An account of another workspace is fine too.
- The row (`MemberInvitation`, table `member_invitations`) works like a client's ([invitations.md](invitations.md)): the address is normalised, the token is a `SecureToken` of which only the SHA-256 is stored, it is valid for 7 days and is saved in the same transaction as the `MemberInvited` event, which the Notifications service turns into the email.
- **The link** is the same `/api/invitations/{token}` as a client's: `InvitationService` looks the hash up in `client_invitations` and then in `member_invitations`, and `GET` answers with `kind` (`Client` or `Member`). `POST accept` (the signed-in account must have the invited address, 403 otherwise) and `POST register` (a new account with that address, signed in at once) make the person a `Member`, mark the invitation used, confirm the account's email and publish `MemberJoined`. A used, cancelled or expired link answers 409; an account that is already a member, or a linked client of the workspace, answers 409.
- **Notifications** ([notifications.md](notifications.md)): `MemberInvited` (the email) and `MemberJoined` (an inbox notification and email for the owner who sent it, "X joined your team", linking to `/w/{id}/team`; skipped if that owner is gone). A client's answer to an approval asked for by a member who has since been removed goes to the owner instead.

## Web app

- **Page**: `app/w/[workspaceId]/team/` (`page.tsx`, `loading.tsx`), linked from the sidebar as "Team" in the "Collaborate" group, in the Calendar's group (`nav.ts`; the client's menu does not have it, and `getWorkspace()` answers a client with a 404). Components in `src/components/team/`, data in `src/lib/team/` (`getMembers`, `getMemberInvitations`).
- **Members** (`MemberList`, client): avatar with initials, name ("(you)" next to your own), email, the day they joined and the role as a badge. For everyone; a member also reads "Only the owner of this workspace can invite or remove people.".
- **The owner** also gets the "Invite member" button (`InviteMemberDialog`: a dialog with one email field, as the sections' adding rule asks), a remove icon on every member but the owner (`UserMinusIcon`, aria-label "Remove {name}", which opens an `AlertDialog` "Remove {name} from the team?"; a 409, such as "still organises 2 upcoming meetings", is shown above the list instead of swallowed) and the "Pending invitations" section (`InvitationList`: the address, when it was sent and when it expires, an "Expired" badge, and icon buttons "Resend" (inviting the same address again, which replaces the open one) and "Cancel"). Only the owner's page asks for the invitations: the API answers a member with 403.
- Every change is one request through `sendJson`, a toast and `router.refresh()`.
- **The invitation page** (`/invite/[token]`) reads `kind` from the preview: for a team invitation it says "{workspace} invited {email} to join their team. Once you accept, you can see all of its clients and projects."; the sign-in, accept and register flows are the same as a client's ([invitations.md](invitations.md)) and lead to `/w/{id}` in both cases.
