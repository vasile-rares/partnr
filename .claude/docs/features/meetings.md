# Meetings and their sync to Google Calendar

Needs a Google connection first: [google-calendar.md](google-calendar.md).

## API: meetings

- `Meeting`, table `meetings`, `MeetingService`; sent to Google by the sync below.
- `/api/workspaces/{id}/meetings` (`POST`, `GET ?includeCancelled=&clientId=&projectId=` soonest first, `GET {id}`, `PUT {id}`, `POST {id}/cancel`; members, no owner-only action). **The list is open to a client too, on the same route**: `MeetingService.ListAsync` answers each side in its own shape (`RoleResponse`: `MeetingResponse` for a member, `ClientMeetingResponse` for a client, whose own meetings only; the filters are ignored for them), and everything else on the route is for members (a client gets 404).
- A meeting has a client (required, never changed), an optional project (of that client, not archived, 409 otherwise) and an **organiser**, the member who created it, fixed for good: any member may edit or cancel it, but its Google event will live in the organiser's calendar.
- **Creating needs the creator's own Google connection to be `Connected`** (409 "Connect your Google account first"; **503 when the Integrations service cannot be reached**, which is asked over gRPC while the person waits); editing and cancelling do not, so a lapsed account cannot lock a meeting. There is no draft.
- The request carries `DateTimeOffset startsAt` / `endsAt` (stored as UTC); a meeting lasts 5 minutes to 8 hours, and a new or moved one has to start in the future (400), while title, note and project can still be fixed while it runs.
- A cancelled meeting and one that already ended are history: update and cancel answer 409, and `Cancel` keeps the row. An archived client or project blocks scheduling and editing (409).
- The client sees only meetings of its own linked, non-archived card (an archived card closes the whole firm's portal door, 404, like for projects), cancelled ones included for 30 days, with the project named **only when the client can open it** (`ListSharedWithClientAsync`: a Planning project is never named); no organiser, no client card (`ClientMeetingResponse`).
- Foreign keys are `Restrict`, as everywhere: a meeting is history.

## API: sync

The sending to Google is the **Integrations service's** (`src/Services/Integrations`, see [google-calendar.md](google-calendar.md)); the meetings service only says what changed and shows the answer. Everything between them is events through RabbitMQ (Wolverine outbox, at least once).

- **Core side** (`Meeting`, `MeetingService`, `MeetingSyncResultService`): every schedule, update and cancel bumps `Version`, makes `SyncStatus` `Pending` and publishes `MeetingChanged` (title, description, times, the client's email, the organiser, the version, whether it is cancelled) in the same transaction as the save. `SyncStatus` (`Pending`, `Synced`, `Failed`, `NeedsReconnect`) and `MeetUrl` are all that is kept; `Version` is also an EF concurrency token (`DbUpdateConcurrencyException` becomes a 409 in `ApplicationDbContext`), so two saves at once cannot undo each other.
- **Integrations side** (`MeetingSync`, table `meeting_syncs`, `MeetingQueueService`, `MeetingSyncService`, `MeetingSyncWorker`, `IGoogleCalendarClient` implemented by `Google/GoogleCalendarClient`, plain `HttpClient`): **the row is the queue** (`Status`, `SyncAttempts`, `NextSyncAtUtc`, `GoogleEventId`, `Version`). `MeetingChanged` queues the row (an event that is not newer than the row changes nothing, so a repeat or one that arrives late is harmless). The worker (every `Google:SyncPollSeconds` = 5 s, off while `Google:ClientId` is empty and unless `Google:Fake` is on) claims due rows with one `UPDATE ... RETURNING ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` that also pushes their next try 5 minutes out, so two workers never take the same row; the claim returns untracked snapshots and the service **loads the row again before it writes the result**.
- The result is applied for the version that was sent: if a newer version arrived meanwhile, the event is kept but the row goes round again (`RecordSynced` / `RecordSyncFailure`), and nothing is told to the meetings service. Only **final** answers are published, as `MeetingSynced` (`Synced`, `Failed`, `NeedsReconnect`, with the version and the Meet link); the meetings service applies it unless the meeting has moved on to a newer version (`Meeting.ApplySyncResult`).
- **The Google event id is the meeting's id** (`MeetingSync.EventId`, 32 hex digits, which Google accepts), so a try whose answer was lost cannot create a second event: the insert answers 409 and the client updates it instead.
- A new event is made with a Meet link (`conferenceData.createRequest`), the client as guest and `sendUpdates=all`, so **Google emails the client the invitation and every change or cancellation**; reminders are the calendar's own (`useDefault`).
- An event the organiser deleted in Google (404 / 410) is never brought back and counts as done; a meeting cancelled before it ever reached Google needs no call.
- **Failures**: `GoogleCalendarException` (down, busy, 5xx, network, 403 for rate limits) backs off 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes, then `Failed` on the 5th try (or at once when the meeting is over); `GoogleAccessRevokedException` (token refresh `invalid_grant`, 401, 403 `insufficientPermissions`) or a missing / unreadable connection makes the row `NeedsReconnect` with no retry and the connection `NeedsReconnect`. `POST .../meetings/{id}/retry-sync` (409 when there is nothing to retry or the meeting is over) queues a `Failed` meeting again: it sets `Pending` here and publishes `MeetingSyncRetryRequested`, which queues the row there.
- **Disconnecting Google** publishes `GoogleDisconnected`: the open `Pending` / `Failed` meetings of the organiser are parked as `NeedsReconnect` on both sides, and the `Synced` ones are left (their events stay in Google); **connecting again** publishes `GoogleConnected`, which puts the `NeedsReconnect` ones that have not ended back in the queue.
- The responses carry `syncStatus` and `meetUrl` (the portal only `meetUrl`, and not for a cancelled meeting).
- **Tests**: Core plays the answers of Integrations by hand (`MeetingSyncResultService`, `FakeGoogleConnectionStatus` for the gRPC check) and `Events/MeetingsOverTheBrokerTests` runs the events over a real RabbitMQ; Integrations tests the queue and the worker with `FakeGoogleCalendarClient` (unit) and the whole loop over a broker (`MeetingsOverTheBrokerTests`); `GoogleCalendarClientTests` checks the real client's requests against a fake HTTP handler.

## Web app

- `app/w/[workspaceId]/meetings/` for both: the page opens the workspace with `getOpenWorkspace` and shows `FirmMeetingsView` to a member and `ClientMeetingsView` to a client; components in `src/components/meetings/`, data in `src/lib/meetings/`, pure rules in `time.ts`.
- The freelancer's page is `ProjectTabs` with **Upcoming** (scheduled and not over) and **Past** (over, or cancelled), each a `FirmMeetingList`: title, a Cancelled badge, the time in the reader's own zone (`MeetingTime`), the client, the project and the length, the notes, then **Join** (the Meet link, in a new tab), **Retry sync** and Edit / Cancel icon buttons (Cancel asks first; a meeting that is over or cancelled has no actions).
- A meeting that is not in step with Google shows a badge while it has not happened: "Sending to Google…" (the list asks the server again every 4 s while one is waiting, so the Meet link turns up on its own), "Couldn't reach Google" with Retry sync, or "Reconnect Google" (a link to `/account`).
- `NewMeetingDialog` (the Meetings page, and the client's page with the client preselected and its upcoming meetings listed under the projects) opens `MeetingForm`: client (fixed when editing), optional project of that client, title, **date picked on a calendar, start time and length** (15 min – 2 h; the end is worked out and the browser's time zone named) and notes; a start in the past is refused in the form and by the API. Without a connected Google the button is a link to `/account`, and `GoogleCalendarNotice` explains why.
- **Times**: the API keeps UTC; `useInBrowser` / `useTimeZone` draw UTC while the server renders and during hydration, then the reader's own zone, so nothing mismatches (`nowIso()` / `isPast()` in `time.ts` keep the clock out of components, which the lint rules require).
- The client's side (`ClientMeetingsView`): **Meetings** in the portal menu, which links to `/w/{id}/meetings` (`ClientMeetingList`: Upcoming with Join, Past with Cancelled), a **Next meeting** card on Home (`ClientNextMeeting`), and a project the client cannot open is never named.
- In e2e tests the browser runs in `Europe/Bucharest` (`test.use({ timezoneId })`), a meeting is made through `POST /api/workspaces/{id}/meetings` and waited for with `expect.poll` until `syncStatus` is `Synced` (the Integrations service's worker runs every 5 s against the fake Google and the answer comes back over RabbitMQ), and its row is `getByRole("main").getByRole("listitem").filter({ hasText })`.
